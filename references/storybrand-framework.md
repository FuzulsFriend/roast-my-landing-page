# StoryBrand Framework — Landing Page Application

Donald Miller's StoryBrand framework applied to landing page copy evaluation. The core idea: the customer is the hero, your product is the guide.

---

## The 7 StoryBrand Elements

### 1. A Character (The Customer)

**What to check:** Does the page start with the customer, not the company?

| Pass | Fail |
|------|------|
| "You spend hours searching for scattered bookmarks" | "We built the most advanced platform" |
| "Managing a remote team is harder than it should be" | "Founded in 2020, our company..." |

**Rule:** The first section should be about the visitor's world — not the company's story.

### 2. Has a Problem

**What to check:** Does the page name a specific problem the customer recognizes?

Three levels:
- **External:** The tangible problem ("I can't find my saved links")
- **Internal:** How it makes them feel ("I feel disorganized and frustrated")
- **Philosophical:** Why it's wrong ("People shouldn't waste time on things that should be simple")

**Rule:** Great pages name all three. Good ones name at least external + internal.

### 3. And Meets a Guide (Your Product)

**What to check:** Does the product position itself as the guide, not the hero?

| Guide language | Hero language |
|---|---|
| "We help you..." | "We are the best..." |
| "Built for teams who..." | "Our award-winning platform..." |
| Shows empathy + competence | Shows off features and awards |

A guide demonstrates: **Empathy** ("We had the same problem") + **Authority** (social proof, credentials).

### 4. Who Gives Them a Plan

**What to check:** Is there a clear, simple plan for how to get started?

| Pass | Fail |
|------|------|
| "3 steps: Sign up, Connect tools, See results" | No explanation of how it works |
| Visual process diagram | "Contact us to learn more" |

**Rule:** 3-4 steps maximum.

### 5. And Calls Them to Action

**Two types:**
1. **Direct CTA** (primary): "Start free trial", "Buy now"
2. **Transitional CTA** (secondary): "Watch demo", "Download guide"

**Rule:** One dominant direct CTA repeated throughout the page.

### 6. That Helps Them Avoid Failure

**What to check:** Does the page show what happens if they DON'T act?

| Pass | Fail |
|------|------|
| "Without this, you'll keep wasting hours on..." | No mention of cost of inaction |
| Shows the pain of the status quo | Only positive messaging |

**Rule:** Mention failure gently — urgency, not manipulation.

### 7. And Ends in Success

**What to check:** Does the page paint a picture of life AFTER using the product?

| Pass | Fail |
|------|------|
| "Imagine opening your laptop and everything is organized" | No vision of the outcome |
| Before/after comparison | Generic "grow your business" |

**Rule:** Success should be specific and emotionally resonant.

## Quick StoryBrand Audit

```
STORYBRAND CHECK:
1. Character (customer first):  [Y/N/Partial]
2. Problem (named clearly):    [Y/N/Partial]
3. Guide (product as helper):  [Y/N/Partial]
4. Plan (3-4 steps):           [Y/N/Partial]
5. CTA (clear and direct):     [Y/N/Partial]
6. Failure (cost of inaction): [Y/N/Partial]
7. Success (outcome vision):   [Y/N/Partial]

Score: X/7 elements present
```

**Scoring impact:**
- 6-7 elements = full points (15 points in Copy dimension)
- 4-5 elements = partial credit (8-12 points)
- 2-3 elements = minimal credit (3-7 points)
- 0-1 elements = zero points

---

## Common Violations

1. **Company as hero:** Page starts with "We" and talks about company history
2. **No problem articulated:** Jumps straight to features
3. **No plan:** Visitor doesn't know how to get started
4. **Competing CTAs:** Multiple CTAs at same prominence
5. **No stakes:** Everything positive, no urgency
6. **Vague success:** "Transform your workflow" instead of specific outcomes

## Using This in the Audit

Maps to **Copy & Messaging** dimension, checkpoint 4 (StoryBrand structure, 15 points).

Also relevant to:
- **First Impression:** Elements 1-2 should be visible above the fold
- **CTA:** Element 5 maps directly
- **Trust:** Elements 3 and 7 (guide credentials + success stories)
