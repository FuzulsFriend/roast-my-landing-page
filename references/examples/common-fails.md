# Common Landing Page Fails

The most frequently encountered issues, organized by frequency.

---

## Top 10 Most Common Issues

### 1. Vague Headline (~70% of pages)
**What:** "Welcome to the Future of [Industry]" or "Empowering Teams Everywhere"
**Impact:** First Impression 60-70, Copy 50-65.
**Fix:** Replace with "[Specific benefit] for [specific audience]"
**Effort:** 10 minutes

### 2. No Social Proof (~60% of pages)
**What:** Zero testimonials, no logos, no user count.
**Impact:** Trust dimension 30-50.
**Fix:** Add 2-3 short testimonials with names + 1 metric.
**Effort:** 1-2 hours

### 3. Feature-First Copy (~55% of pages)
**What:** "AI-powered analytics", "Real-time sync engine"
**Impact:** Copy dimension 55-70.
**Fix:** For each feature, ask "so what?" and lead with the answer.
**Effort:** 1-2 hours

### 4. Weak CTA (~50% of pages)
**What:** "Submit", "Get Started", "Learn More" with no context.
**Impact:** CTA dimension 60-75.
**Fix:** Make specific: "Start my free trial" or "Get my report"
**Effort:** 5 minutes

### 5. Mobile Afterthought (~45% of pages)
**What:** Desktop squished onto mobile. Tiny text, small buttons.
**Impact:** Mobile dimension 40-60.
**Fix:** Responsive redesign with mobile-first approach.
**Effort:** 4-8 hours

### 6. No "How It Works" (~45% of pages)
**What:** No explanation of the process.
**Impact:** Trust drops 10-15 points.
**Fix:** Add a 3-4 step "How It Works" section.
**Effort:** 1-2 hours

### 7. Wall of Text (~40% of pages)
**What:** Long paragraphs, no headers or bullets.
**Impact:** Copy drops 10-15 points.
**Fix:** Break into short paragraphs, add headers, use bullets.
**Effort:** 30-60 minutes

### 8. Slow Loading (~35% of pages)
**What:** 4+ seconds to load.
**Impact:** Performance dimension 50-70.
**Fix:** Compress images, lazy-load, minimize scripts.
**Effort:** 2-4 hours

### 9. Too Many CTAs (~30% of pages)
**What:** "Sign up", "Book demo", "Watch video" all at equal prominence.
**Impact:** CTA dimension 50-65.
**Fix:** Pick ONE primary action. Everything else subordinate.
**Effort:** 30 minutes

### 10. No Risk Reversal (~40% of pages)
**What:** No free trial, no guarantee, no "cancel anytime."
**Impact:** Trust drops 15-20 points.
**Fix:** Add "14-day free trial" or "30-day money-back guarantee" near CTA.
**Effort:** 15 minutes

## Fails by Dimension

### First Impression Killers
- Auto-playing video with sound (-15 points)
- Popup before visitor reads anything (-10 points)
- Generic stock photo hero
- Company logo larger than headline

### Copy Killers
- Company-first copy ("We are the leading...")
- Buzzword soup
- "Click here" links (-5 per occurrence)
- No specificity ("save time", "grow business")

### CTA Killers
- CTA only at page bottom
- Ghost button as primary CTA
- "Submit" as button text
- No CTA above the fold

### Trust Killers
- Fake testimonials (stock photos, no specifics)
- Fake countdown timer
- No FAQ section

### Mobile Killers
- Horizontal scrolling
- Body text under 16px
- Touch targets under 44px

### Technical Killers
- No HTTPS
- Missing meta tags
- Multiple H1 tags
- Broken links
