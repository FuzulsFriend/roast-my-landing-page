# What an A+ Landing Page Looks Like (Score 90+)

Reference patterns that earn top scores across all 6 dimensions.

---

## Above the Fold (First Impression: 90+)

A 90+ first impression has ALL of these:

1. **Headline passes the 3-second test:** A stranger immediately knows what the product does, who it's for, and why it's different.
   - Example: "Email marketing for creators who hate marketing — send beautiful emails in 5 minutes"

2. **Visual hierarchy guides the eye:** Headline largest. Subhead supports. One CTA is the most colorful element.

3. **Complete above-fold:** Headline + subhead + CTA + trust signal (logo bar, user count, or short testimonial) all visible without scrolling.

4. **Professional design:** Consistent colors (2-3), aligned grid, whitespace, real product screenshot.

5. **Differentiation signal:** Something memorable — a specific number, unusual angle, or bold claim.

---

## Copy & Messaging (Score 90+)

1. Headline is specific and benefit-driven. Zero buzzwords.
2. StoryBrand flow: Problem -> Guide -> Plan -> CTA -> Failure -> Success.
3. Zero jargon. A smart 14-year-old could understand every sentence.
4. Benefits lead, features support: "Find any file in 2 seconds (powered by AI search)"
5. Specific claims backed by evidence: "12,000 teams" not "thousands of users"
6. Scannable: short paragraphs, clear headers, bullet points.

---

## CTA (Score 90+)

1. One dominant CTA repeated 3+ times on the page.
2. Specific button copy: "Start my free 14-day trial" not "Sign up."
3. High contrast button that passes the squint test.
4. Supporting text: "No credit card required. Cancel anytime."
5. Secondary CTA clearly subordinate (ghost button or text link).

---

## Trust & Social Proof (Score 90+)

1. Named testimonials with photos and specific results.
2. Logo bar above the fold with 4-6 recognizable companies.
3. Specific numbers: user count, results metrics.
4. Risk reversal visible near the CTA.
5. FAQ section addressing top 5 objections.
6. "How it works" section with 3-4 steps.

---

## Mobile & Accessibility (Score 90+)

1. Intentionally designed for mobile — not squished desktop.
2. All touch targets 44x44px+ with spacing.
3. Body text 16px+ on mobile.
4. WCAG AA contrast on all text.
5. Meaningful alt text on all images.
6. Keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators.
7. Proper heading hierarchy (one H1, sequential levels).

---

## Performance & Technical (Score 90+)

1. Loads in under 2 seconds (LCP under 2.5s).
2. Proper meta tags: unique title, meta description, OG tags.
3. Semantic HTML: header, main, nav, footer.
4. Zero broken links and images.
5. No console errors.
6. HTTPS with valid certificate.
7. No layout shift (CLS under 0.1).

---

## The "Perfect" SaaS Landing Page Structure

```
1. HERO: Specific headline + subhead + CTA + product screenshot
2. LOGO BAR: "Trusted by" with 4-6 logos
3. PROBLEM: 2-3 pain points with emotional hooks
4. SOLUTION: How the product solves each pain point
5. HOW IT WORKS: 3-4 numbered steps
6. SOCIAL PROOF: 2-3 testimonials with results
7. PRICING: Clear tiers, popular plan highlighted
8. FAQ: 5-7 objection-addressing questions
9. FINAL CTA: Repeat primary CTA + value summary
10. FOOTER: Navigation, legal, social links
```

## Using This in the Audit

Compare against these patterns:
- Matches most = 90+ for that dimension
- Matches some = 75-89
- Misses most = below 75
